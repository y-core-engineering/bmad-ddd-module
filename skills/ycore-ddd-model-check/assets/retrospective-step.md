ycore-ddd domain model check: once the epic under retrospective is determined and confirmed, resolve `modules.ycore-ddd.review_folder` with `resolve_config.py` and look for `domain-model-review-epic-<epic>.md` in it. If it is missing, invoke the `ycore-ddd-model-check` skill headless for this epic and use the report it writes. Read the report's tally and its `## Decisions` table. A finding of severity `high` without a decision, or a report whose frontmatter is not `status: final`, means the epic is not accepted: name those findings in the verdict and offer to decide them with `ycore-ddd-model-check`. Context-boundary findings belong in the retrospective's discussion of what the next epic can build on.
